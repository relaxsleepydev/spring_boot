# Basics of Domain Name

> **Domain Name** is a unique, easy-to-remember address used to visit websites such as google.com

> Domain names are managed by **domain registries**, which delegates the reservation of domain names to registrars.

> **URL (Uniform Resource Locator)** contains the domain name of a site as well as other informations like protocol and the path.

> **Parts of a Domain Name** are upto two or three, each separated by a dot, where the rightmost part is called Top-Level Domain, to its left is called 2LD (second-level domain) and if there is anything in its left, it will be called 3LD(third-level domain).

> **How to keep a domain name secure**: To avoid bad practices like the registrar buying your domain and selling you back at exorbitant price, choose trustworthy registrars like cloudflare ones.
